The proposal

The idea

Bring production in-house. Bidding, budgets, planning and delivery, all under one roof, run by House of Errors instead of handed out to a different production company every time.

We’d only go outside the business where it genuinely has to, specialist kit, a very niche skillset, insurance only a third party can give you. Everything else sits with us, with one person owning it end to end.

The case

Why it’s worth doing

You’re not starting cold

This isn’t a roster built from nothing. It’s the crew, studios and suppliers I already work with across other jobs, married together under House of Errors instead of hired fresh each time.

No markup in the middle

An outside production company usually adds markup, insurance and a separate producer’s fee, about £41k on last year’s spend. That stops being money that leaves the business.

You plan further ahead

One team that can see the whole calendar plans a campaign months out, instead of scrambling once a producer gets briefed in at the last minute.

You see where every pound goes

One set of books, one tracker, nothing sitting between the budget and what it was actually spent on.

Nothing walks out the door

Rates, contacts and what’s worked before stay with the business, instead of leaving with whichever freelance producer wrapped the job.

It scales either way

The same team runs a flagship campaign or a quick social job, no re-tendering whatever the size.
